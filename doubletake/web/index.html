Wi-Fi
Cable
FSDC: Field Station Direct Connect
When a network cable finds no network, as when it is plugged straight into a laptop, the tablet links to the laptop by itself after about 20 seconds. The top bar shows Linking, then FSDC. On the laptop, open smb://doubletake.local. Plugged into a router or the dock, the cable works as usual.
Wi-Fi networks
Tap a network to join it. A password is asked for only the first time.
Hotspot for the laptop
The tablet makes its own Wi-Fi network for the laptop to join. While it is on, the tablet is not on any other Wi-Fi network.

Dark screen between backups
A minute after an unattended backup, the screen goes dark. The tablet stays awake: plugging in the camera, or a touch, lights it again.
Backup beeps
One beep as a backup starts, two when it is done, three low ones when it needs a look. Stopped by hand: no beep.
What to copy
Shoots are deleted in the shoot library, never by themselves, and not within a day of being copied. The camera's cards are never touched: format them in the camera.
